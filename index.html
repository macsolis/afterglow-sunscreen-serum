☀️The Glow Story

Afterglow was created out of the desire to make sun protection both effective and elegant. It is also inspired by one Taylor Swift for aour swiftes. Our journey began with the realization that most sunscreens left a heavy, greasy feeling on the skin, makin people uncomfortable from using them daily. We wanted to create a product that feels more like skincare than sunscreen. Our product, the Afterglow Sunscreen Serum, is a lightweight SPF 60 PA ++++ formula that protects against harmful UV rays while nourishing the skin with antioxidants and hydrating ingredients. Unlike Other sunscreens, it doubles as a serum, offering beauty and protection in one bottle. Entering the skincare industry was not easy. The market is filled with established giants, strict regulations, and the need for constant innovation. However, Afterglow overcame these barriers through unique branding, innovative formulation, and listening to consumer needs/demands for simplicity and elegance. We face competition from trusted skincare brands such as Neutrogena, belo, and Cetapil but Despite this, Afterglow stands out by blending sun care with skincare, offering a multi-functional product for modern lifestyles. Our focus on both performance and quality that make us unique. Our price point of Php 300 per bottle strikes the perfect balance between affordability and quality. Consumers can trust they are receiving excellent protection and skincare benefits at a fair price, making Afterglow the smart, quality , and stylish choice for daily sun protection.
